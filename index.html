Server rules

Simple rules that keep the server fair and fun for everyone.

1

No hacked clients or cheats

Kill aura, auto crystal, x-ray, fly, and similar mods are not allowed. Cosmetic and performance mods are fine.

2

No bug abuse or duping

Exploiting glitches or duplicating items and currency is banned. Report bugs to staff instead.

3

Respect other players

No harassment, hate speech, threats, or doxxing. Keep chat friendly.

4

No combat logging

Leaving during a fight to avoid dying counts as combat logging and will be punished.

5

No real-money trading

Do not sell in-game items, currency, or accounts for real money.

6

No alt abuse

Do not use alternate accounts to farm rewards, dodge bans, or gain an unfair advantage.

7

No spam or advertising

Do not spam chat or advertise other servers, channels, or products.

8

Follow staff instructions

Staff decisions are final in game. Do not evade punishments. Appeal them on Discord.

Breaking a rule can lead to a mute, kick, or ban depending on how serious it is. Staff may update these rules at any time, so check back often. Questions? Ask in our Discord.
